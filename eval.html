TrialMatch Evaluation

Tests four ranking systems on the same gold set of patient cases with blind labels: No AI (ClinicalTrials.gov's own order), v1 (AI similarity on condition and story, the original app), v2 (AI similarity on the full profile plus eligibility rules) and v3 (v2 plus expanded eligibility rules built from v2's error analysis, the current app).

Gold set: patient cases

Six realistic patient profiles that cover different situations: metastatic vs early disease, new vs relapsed, biomarkers, brain metastases, non-cancer conditions and pediatrics.

Build the labeling pool

For each case, this pulls the same 50 recruiting trials the app sees, freezes them (so results are reproducible even as trials change), runs all three systems, and pools each system's top 5 into a labeling queue. The AI model runs in your browser.

Label trials

Labeling guide: read once before you start
  • Good match (2): the trial is for this patient's condition and situation, and nothing you can see in the criteria rules them out.
  • Right disease, but likely excluded (1): relevant condition, but a criterion probably rules them out (stage, prior treatment, biomarker, brain metastases, age…).
  • Not relevant (0): wrong condition, healthy-volunteer study, or wouldn't help this patient.
  • Treat the patient's listed biomarkers and treatments as complete: if a trial requires a mutation or prior drug that isn't listed, label it 1. Details the profile doesn't ask about (histology, lab values, imaging) are assumed fine.
  • Observational or testing studies the patient qualifies for count as a good match (2).
  • You can't see which system ranked a trial, or its score. That keeps labels unbiased.
  • Keyboard: 2, 1, 0 to label · ← / → to move.

Validating the eligibility rules

Among trials labeled relevant (1 or 2): when the rules flag a ✕ likely conflict, did the labeler also judge the patient excluded? Recall = share of excluded trials the rules caught.

Iterate: tune v3's weights

Move the sliders and v3 re-ranks instantly (v2 stays frozen at the app's original weights). If new, unlabeled trials enter a top 5, label them in step 2 so the comparison stays fair.
