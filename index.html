Describe a diagnosis in your own words. TrialMatch pulls trials that are recruiting now from ClinicalTrials.gov, ranks them with an AI language model, and translates the eligibility fine print into plain English.

Data: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 (U.S. National Library of Medicine). Ranking model: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence embeddings via Transformers.js, running entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere except the search terms to ClinicalTrials.gov.
