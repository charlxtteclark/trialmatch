How the matching works
  1. Search. Pulls up to 50 trials that are recruiting now for your condition and location from ClinicalTrials.gov.
  2. AI similarity. An AI language model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) turns your description and each trial's title, conditions, interventions and summary into vectors and measures how close they are in meaning (cosine similarity). This is the base score.
  3. Eligibility checks. Rules read each trial's inclusion and exclusion criteria and compare them to your details. Each finding is marked ✓ fits, ✕ likely conflict or ? check with the study team, and shows the exact criterion it came from.
  4. Final fit score = AI similarity × 0.55 for each conflict × 0.92 for each item to check, + 4 points for each detail that fits (up to +12).
  5. Preferences (phase, placebo, nearby sites) hide trials rather than re-rank them.

Rules read free text, so they can miss or misread criteria. A ✕ means "probably not", not "definitely not". Always confirm with the study team.

Data: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 (U.S. National Library of Medicine). Ranking model: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence embeddings via Transformers.js, running entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere except the condition and location to ClinicalTrials.gov.
Not medical advice. Always review trials with your care team. Eligibility is decided by the study team.
