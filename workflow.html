How TrialMatch works

The full workflow from a patient's question to a trial enrollment, what the AI decides on its own, and where a person has to approve.

Data AI / automated Human decision or approval
1
Human

Patient or caregiver describes the situation

Condition, location, age, plus optional stage, diagnosis date, biomarkers, treatments, activity level, other conditions and preferences.

Decision: what to share. Everything stays in the browser; only condition and location leave the device.
2
Data

Search ClinicalTrials.gov

Pulls up to 50 recruiting trials for the condition and location (free public API).

3
AI

Rank by meaning

A small language model (MiniLM, in the browser) compares the patient's profile with each trial and scores similarity.

AI decides: how relevant each trial is to the description.
4
AI + rules

Check eligibility

Rules read inclusion and exclusion criteria and mark each patient detail ✓ fits, ✕ likely conflict, or ? check with the study team, quoting the exact criterion.

Decides: which trials look likely to exclude the patient, and pushes them down. Never decides: that someone is eligible.
5
AI

Fit score, shortlist and plain-English explanation

Fit = similarity × penalties for conflicts + small bonus for details that fit. Shows "Why this rank", decoded jargon, nearby sites and contacts.

Patient decides: preference filters (skip Phase 1, skip placebo, nearby only, hide conflicts).
6
Approval 1 · Patient

Patient chooses which trials to pursue

Reviews the shortlist and the ✕ / ? items, and picks trials worth asking about.

7
Approval 2 · Doctor

Care team reviews the shortlist

The oncologist or physician confirms a trial is medically appropriate and fills in what the patient may not know (biomarkers, histology, labs).

8
Approval 3 · Study team

Study coordinator screens and enrolls

The only party that decides eligibility. Formal screening, informed consent, enrollment.

↻ Improvement loop (behind the scenes)

Freeze a gold set of patient cases → label each system's top 5 blind (Approval 4 · reviewer) → score Good@5, nDCG, Excluded@5, MRR → study the misses → change rules or weights → re-label new trials that surface → Approval 5 · developer ships only if scores improve. See the results →
